What can this system do?

Local keyword search with limited English and Chinese query patterns. Models are matched exactly. Complex modifications, external service bulletins, and unsupported conditions are not evaluated automatically. Results are limited and do not list every applicable directive.

Optional AI turns your question into structured fields. Every value it returns must appear in your own text or it is discarded. Only your question is sent to the model, never the documents, and applicability is always decided by deterministic rules.

Citations identify CSV fields and character ranges. Published page numbers cover the document, not an exact claim page. No expert review or legal-effect verification has been performed.
